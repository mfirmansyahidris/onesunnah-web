One Sunnah — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 21 October 2026

Contact: manca.fi@gmail.com
Application: fi.dev.onesunnah (Android)

Reading without an account
One Sunnah does not require an account. You can read daily hadith, use the
home-screen widget, and save favourites without providing your name or email.
There are no advertisements or in-app purchases in this version.

Data stored on your device
The downloaded hadith collection, favourites, language, daily selection, widget
preferences, notification preference, and diagnostics preference are stored on
your device. Favourites and reading text are not uploaded as a personal library.
Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this local data. The app disables
Android cloud backup for its data.

Cloud content and notifications
Google Cloud Firestore supplies published content. Requests expose connection
information, such as an IP address and technical request metadata, to the service
provider. Internet access is needed for the first download and later updates.
When you enable notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging uses an app installation
identifier, a registration token, app/device metadata, and topic membership to
deliver reminders. You can turn notifications off in Settings; the app then
unsubscribes and requests deletion of its messaging token. Delivery depends on
Android, connectivity, and the service sending reminders.

Optional diagnostics in version 1.0.0 and later
Analytics, crash-report transmission, and performance reporting are off by
default. Starting with version 1.0.0 (build 15), optional diagnostics require a
neutral, local age-group check and a fresh opt-in by a user aged 18 or older.
The selected age group is not sent to a server; only the adult diagnostics
consent is retained on the device. Younger or unknown-age readers cannot enable
diagnostics and can continue reading without it. A previous diagnostics setting
without this adult check does not enable collection in build 15.
If you enable diagnostics in Settings, Google Analytics for Firebase,
Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Performance Monitoring can process app
interactions, installation/session identifiers, crash logs, device/app details,
timing and network-performance measurements. Connection data may be used to
derive coarse country/region information. We do not request location permission
or GPS coordinates. These data help measure usage and fix reliability problems.
Custom analytics events do not include hadith text, your name, or your email.
Advertising identifier collection and advertising-personalisation consent are
disabled in version 1.0.0 and later, including when diagnostics are enabled.

Earlier internal-test versions
Versions before 1.0.0 initialise Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance
Monitoring automatically and do not include the diagnostics switch described
above. Those SDKs may collect the corresponding usage, diagnostic, installation,
device, network, approximate-location and advertising-identifier data under their
default settings. This distinction applies to internal-test version 0.6.4.

Controls and retention
In version 1.0.0 and later, turning diagnostics off stops subsequent diagnostic
transmission, resets local Analytics data, and requests removal of unsent crash
reports. Turning a feature off does not automatically erase records already
received by a provider. Previously transmitted data remain subject to the
retention settings and deletion capabilities of the relevant Firebase/Analytics
service. No fixed retention period is claimed here. For a privacy or deletion
request, email manca.fi@gmail.com; do not post personal information publicly.
We may need relevant technical identifiers to locate records because the Android
app has no user accounts. Clearing local storage does not erase provider records.

Providers, sharing and security
Firebase/Google provides content hosting, messaging, analytics and diagnostics.
Data are transmitted over HTTPS. We do not sell personal data. Service providers
may process data outside your country, including through their subprocessors,
according to their applicable terms. We have not implemented independent
end-to-end encryption or obtained an independent security certification.
Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

External links and sharing
Opening a source sends you to that publisher in your browser, where its own
privacy policy applies. Sharing a hadith uses the app you choose through Android;
you control the recipient and the text being shared.

Children and changes
One Sunnah does not offer a child account or ask children to submit contact
details. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact
the email above. Changes to this policy will carry a new date and will be made
available with the app and its published privacy-policy page.
